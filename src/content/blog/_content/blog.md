    hint: "Try lighting both ends of one rope at the same time."
    answer: "1. Light Rope A at both ends and Rope B at one end — simultaneously.\n\n2. Rope A will burn in 30 minutes, because lighting both ends makes it burn twice as fast (unevenness doesn't matter).\n\n3. When Rope A is completely burnt (after 30 minutes), immediately light the other end of Rope B as well.\n\n4. At this point, Rope B has been burning from one end for 30 minutes — so it has 30 minutes' worth of burn left, but unevenly distributed.\n\n5. Lighting the other end causes the remaining rope to burn in 15 minutes (again, it burns from both ends).\n\n6. When Rope B finishes burning, a total of 45 minutes has passed."
